The problem

Ayurveda has good software. Just not this piece of it.

Private clinic EMRs already digitize prescriptions and billing. None of them turn case-taking into standardized, government-usable data.

Government platforms

A-HMIS · NAMASTE · Ayush Grid

  • ABHA-linked hospital records
  • Standard diagnosis codes exist
  • No structured case-taking workflow — codes have to be applied after the fact
Private clinic EMRs

Clinicea · Healthray · AyurGrid

  • Case-taking captured as free-text notes
  • Great for solo/private practice billing
  • Paid SaaS — not standardized for govt hospitals
Teaching hospitals today

What's actually used

  • Paper case-sheets, still
  • No two hospitals record the same way
  • Data unsearchable, unusable for research

The gap: nobody has built the free, standards-compliant, ABHA/NAMASTE-linked case-taking layer that government Ayush hospitals can actually adopt at scale. That's what this is.

The solution

Seven features. One goal: paper's clinical rigor, digital's traceability.

Nothing here replaces how a Vaidya thinks — it structures what they already ask.

1

Structured Case Sheet

Prakriti, Nidan Panchaka and Ashtavidha Pariksha as guided fields, not blank paragraphs.

2

Photo-to-Scan Intake

Snap or upload a photo of an existing paper case sheet or prescription — OCR lifts the patient's details straight into the digital form.

3

NAMASTE / ICD-11 Auto-Coding

Diagnosis selections map to national codes automatically, in the background.

4

ABHA ID Linking

The case record follows the patient across visits and institutions.

5

Voice-to-Text Entry

Speak the case instead of typing — OPD speed stays intact.

6

Ministry-Level Dashboard

Aggregate dosha and disease-pattern data, usable for real research.

7

Multilingual Forms

Regional-language practitioners aren't left out of digitization.

Being honest

Challenges we expect — and how we handle them

Click any row to see the mitigation.

Doctor adoption resistance

▾
Why it's real

Senior doctors are used to free-form paper notes; rigid fields may feel restrictive.

Our mitigation

Hybrid fields — structured selections plus an optional free-text remarks box on every section.

Poor rural connectivity

▾
Why it's real

Many Ayush centers have weak or unreliable internet access.

Our mitigation

Offline-first app that syncs to ABHA/NAMASTE automatically once connected.

Loss of clinical nuance

▾
Why it's real

Fixed dropdowns can oversimplify subtle diagnostic judgment.

Our mitigation

A remarks field on every section preserves the doctor's own judgment alongside the structured data.

Training & onboarding cost

▾
Why it's real

Staff need time to learn any new system, taking time from patient care.

Our mitigation

Phased rollout starting with teaching hospitals, plus a guided in-app tutorial for students and faculty.

Data privacy (DPDP Act)

▾
Why it's real

ABHA-linked health data demands strict compliance and careful handling.

Our mitigation

End-to-end encryption, role-based access control, and consent-first design from day one.
